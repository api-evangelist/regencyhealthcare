Loss of appetite refers to a reduced desire to eat or a complete lack of interest in food. While everyone may occasionally skip a meal due to stress or a busy schedule, persistent loss of appetite should not be ignored. It may indicate an underlying medical condition, infection, digestive disorder, mental health concern, or nutritional deficiency.
