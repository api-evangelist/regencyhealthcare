Paralysis is a serious neurological condition that causes partial or complete loss of muscle movement in one or more parts of the body. It usually occurs when the communication between the brain, spinal cord, and muscles is interrupted due to injury or disease. Depending on the underlying cause, paralysis may develop suddenly as seen in stroke or gradually because of progressive neurological disorders.
